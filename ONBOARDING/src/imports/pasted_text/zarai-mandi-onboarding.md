### Zarai Mandi – Onboarding Flow and Existing Customer Flow

### 1. New User Onboarding Flow

The onboarding process is designed to collect the user's basic preferences, product interests, location, subscription requirements, and account information before giving access to the personalized Zarai Mandi dashboard.

The subscription pricing is product/category-based. The price depends on the products or services selected by the customer during onboarding.

### Screen 1 – Language Selection

The first screen allows the user to select their preferred language.

The available language options are:

* English

* Urdu

* Sindhi

* Punjabi

* Balochi

* Pashtu

* Saraiki

The purpose of this screen is to personalize the application interface according to the user's preferred language.

Once the language is selected, the user continues to the next onboarding step.

### Screen 2 – Product Interest

The second screen asks the user which agricultural products, commodities, or related services they are interested in.

The user can search for products and select multiple items.

Examples include:

* Cotton

* Mustard

* Rice

* Wheat

* Maize

* Pulses

* Sesame

* Spices

* Millet

* Paddy

* Dates

* Sugarcane/Sugar

* Edible Oil

* Fertilizer

* Livestock

* Fruits

* Vegetables

* Dry Fruit

* Herbs

* Kiryana

* Live Market

* Mills

The user can select multiple products according to their requirements.

The selected products are displayed under a "Your Selections" section so the user can review or remove products before continuing.

The purpose of this step is to determine which commodity prices, market information, alerts, insights, and services should be prioritized for the user.

### Product-Based Pricing

The selected products also determine the applicable subscription pricing.

Products are divided into different pricing categories.

### Standard Product Category – PKR 3,000/month

The following products fall under the PKR 3,000 monthly category:

* Wheat

* Maize

* Sesame

* Millet

* Cotton

* Paddy

* Rice

* Dates

* Mustard

* Spices

* Pulses

* Sugarcane/Sugar

* Dry Fruit

* Live Market

Pricing for this category is:

1 Month: PKR 3,000

3 Months: PKR 8,100 10% discount

6 Months: PKR 15,300 15% discount

1 Year: PKR 27,000 25% discount

The longer the subscription period, the greater the overall discount.

### Premium Product Category – PKR 5,000/month

The following products fall under the PKR 5,000 monthly category:

* Edible Oil

* Fertilizer

* Livestock

* Kiryana

* Fruits

* Vegetables

Pricing for this category is:

1 Month: PKR 5,000

3 Months: PKR 13,500 10% discount

6 Months: PKR 25,500 15% discount

1 Year: PKR 45,000 25% discount

### Mills

Mills are included as a product/service category, but the provided pricing sheet does not specify a subscription price for Mills.

The Mills price should therefore be configured separately once the applicable pricing is finalized.

### Multiple Product Selection

If a customer selects products from different pricing categories, the system should determine the applicable subscription price based on the selected products.

For example, if the user selects only Wheat, Rice, and Pulses, the customer falls under the PKR 3,000/month category.

If the user selects products such as Fruits, Vegetables, Livestock, or Fertilizer, the PKR 5,000/month category becomes applicable.

The system should clearly communicate the resulting price before the customer confirms the subscription.

### Screen 3 – Location Selection

The third screen collects the user's location.

The application provides two options.

### Auto-Detect Location

The user can allow Zarai Mandi to use the device's location/GPS to automatically determine their location.

### Manual Location Selection

If the user does not want to use automatic detection, they can manually select:

* Province

* District

* City

For example:

Punjab → Lahore → Lahore

The purpose of this screen is to make sure the customer receives mandi prices, market information, and services relevant to their geographical location.

Location can also be used to personalize the market information shown for the products selected by the customer.

### Screen 4 – Confirm Location

After the location is detected, the application displays the detected location to the user.

For example:

Location Detected!

Lahore, Punjab

The user is asked:

"Is this correct?"

The screen also confirms that the location was detected using GPS.

The user has two choices:

Change Location – The user can go back and select a different location.

Yes, Continue – The user confirms the detected location and proceeds.

This step prevents incorrect location information from being saved to the user's profile.

### Screen 5 – Subscription Plans

The fifth screen presents the subscription options based on the products selected by the customer.

The subscription is not a single fixed price for every customer. The monthly price depends on the selected product/category.

The application should show the applicable pricing clearly after the customer has selected their products.

### PKR 3,000/month Category

For products such as:

* Wheat

* Maize

* Sesame

* Millet

* Cotton

* Paddy

* Rice

* Dates

* Mustard

* Spices

* Pulses

* Sugarcane/Sugar

* Dry Fruit

* Live Market

The available subscription periods are:

* 1 Month – PKR 3,000

* 3 Months – PKR 8,100

* 6 Months – PKR 15,300

* 1 Year – PKR 27,000

### PKR 5,000/month Category

For products such as:

* Edible Oil

* Fertilizer

* Livestock

* Kiryana

* Fruits

* Vegetables

The available subscription periods are:

* 1 Month – PKR 5,000

* 3 Months – PKR 13,500

* 6 Months – PKR 25,500

* 1 Year – PKR 45,000

### Subscription Discounts

The pricing structure provides discounts for customers who choose longer subscription periods.

The discount structure is:

* 1 Month: No discount

* 3 Months: 10% discount

* 6 Months: 15% discount

* 1 Year: 25% discount

The subscription screen should show the original monthly rate, selected duration, applicable discount, and final payable amount.

This allows the customer to understand exactly how much they are paying before proceeding.

### Screen 6 – Free Trial Information

The sixth screen explains the free trial and what happens after the trial period.

New users receive a 3-day free trial.

During the trial period, the customer can access the applicable premium features associated with their selected products and subscription plan.

The screen should communicate:

* Full access to applicable premium features during the trial

* No subscription charge during the 3-day trial

* Ability to cancel before the trial ends

* Reminder before the trial ends

* Trial end date

* Selected subscription plan

* Applicable product/category

* Subscription price that will apply after the trial

For example, if the customer selected Wheat and chose a 3-month subscription, the system should clearly show the applicable 3-month price of PKR 8,100 and the 10% discount.

If the customer selected a PKR 5,000 category product and selected 1 year, the system should show PKR 45,000 with the applicable 25% discount.

The user then selects Continue to Account to proceed with account creation.

### Screen 7 – Account Details

The seventh screen is used to create the user's account.

The user provides:

* Full Name

* Phone Number

* Email Address (optional)

The user must also agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

The user then selects Continue.

If the user already has an account, there is also a Login option so they can skip the new-user onboarding process.

The purpose of this step is to create the user's Zarai Mandi account and connect their language, product interests, location, subscription, and pricing information with their account.

### Screen 8 – Phone Verification

The eighth screen verifies the user's phone number.

A 6-digit OTP is sent to the phone number entered during account creation.

The user enters the OTP into the verification fields.

The screen also provides:

* The phone number being verified

* An option to change the phone number

* A countdown before another OTP can be requested

* A verification button

The purpose of this step is to verify the user's phone number and secure the account.

Once the OTP is successfully verified, the user continues to the final onboarding screen.

### Screen 9 – Onboarding Complete

The final screen confirms that the onboarding process has been completed successfully.

The user is welcomed to Zarai Mandi and informed that their personalized dashboard is ready.

The screen confirms that:

* Their preferred language has been saved

* Their selected product interests have been saved

* Their location has been set

* Their applicable subscription category has been determined

* Their subscription duration and pricing have been saved

* Their 3-day free trial has been activated

The user then selects Go to Dashboard.

They are taken to the main Zarai Mandi dashboard, where the application can provide personalized mandi prices, market information, alerts, insights, and other services based on their selected products and location.

### 2. Existing Customer Flow

Existing customers should not have to repeat the entire onboarding process.

The system recognizes that the customer already has an account and takes them through a much shorter authentication flow.

Their previously saved language, products, location, subscription, and pricing information remain associated with their account.

### Step 1 – Login

The existing user selects Login instead of going through the new-user onboarding process.

The user provides their existing login information, primarily their registered phone number.

The system identifies the existing customer account.

The customer does not need to select their language, products, location, or subscription again.

### Step 2 – Phone/OTP Verification

For security, the existing user receives an OTP on their registered phone number.

The user enters the OTP to verify their identity.

Once the OTP is successfully verified, the customer does not need to repeat:

* Language selection

* Product selection

* Location selection

* Subscription selection

* Pricing selection

* Account creation

All previously saved information remains connected to the customer's account.

If the customer's subscription is still active, the system should continue using the existing subscription and its applicable pricing.

### Step 3 – Personalized Dashboard

After successful verification, the existing customer goes directly to their personalized dashboard.

Their previously saved information remains available, including:

* Preferred language

* Selected agricultural products

* Saved location

* Subscription category

* Subscription duration

* Subscription start/end date

* Applicable pricing

* Existing preferences and settings

The dashboard can immediately show information relevant to that customer.

For example, if a customer previously selected Rice, Wheat, and Pulses and their location is Lahore, Punjab, the dashboard can prioritize relevant mandi prices and market information for those products in that location.

If the customer has subscribed to a longer plan, such as 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year, the system should also retain the corresponding subscription period and discounted price.

### 3. Subscription Pricing Logic

The pricing system is based on the product category selected by the customer.

The standard category is priced at:

PKR 3,000/month

The higher-priced category is:

PKR 5,000/month

The subscription duration determines the discount.

For the PKR 3,000 category:

1 Month: PKR 3,000

3 Months: PKR 8,100 — 10% discount

6 Months: PKR 15,300 — 15% discount

1 Year: PKR 27,000 — 25% discount

For the PKR 5,000 category:

1 Month: PKR 5,000

3 Months: PKR 13,500 — 10% discount

6 Months: PKR 25,500 — 15% discount

1 Year: PKR 45,000 — 25% discount

The pricing should be shown dynamically according to the customer's selected products.

This means the customer should not be shown irrelevant subscription prices. The application should first understand what products the customer is interested in and then display the applicable subscription pricing.

### 4. Difference Between New and Existing Customers

### New Customers

New customers follow the complete journey:

Language Selection → Product Interest → Location Selection → Location Confirmation → Product-Based Subscription Pricing → Free Trial Information → Account Creation → OTP Verification → Dashboard

The purpose is to collect all the information required to personalize the customer's experience and determine the correct subscription pricing.

The system determines the customer's applicable pricing category based on their selected products.

### Existing Customers

Existing customers follow a shorter journey:

Login → OTP Verification → Personalized Dashboard

Their previously saved preferences and subscription information are retained.

They do not need to repeat:

Language → Products → Location → Subscription → Pricing → Account Creation

The customer only needs to authenticate their account before accessing the dashboard.

### 5. Overall Objective of the Flow

The overall onboarding experience is designed to make Zarai Mandi personalized from the moment a new customer enters the application.

The system collects the customer's:

Language + Product Interests + Location + Subscription Duration

The selected products determine the applicable subscription pricing category.

The subscription duration then determines the applicable discount.

The system therefore creates a personalized subscription based on the customer's actual requirements rather than showing the same price to every customer.

The 3-day free trial allows new customers to experience the applicable premium features before the selected subscription becomes active.

For existing customers, the application focuses on fast and secure login, allowing them to reach their personalized dashboard without repeating the onboarding process.

The overall flow can therefore be summarized as:

New Customer:

Language → Products → Location → Confirm Location → Determine Pricing → Select Duration → 3-Day Trial → Account → OTP → Dashboard

Existing Customer:

Login → OTP → Existing Preferences & Subscription Retrieved → Dashboard
